# HOT Portal

[portal.hotosm.org](https://portal.hotosm.org) brings HOT's tools together
and helps coordinate projects across them.

## What it does

- **One login for all tools:** HOT Accounts, plus a shared tool menu.
- **See your work across tools:** sections for Imagery, Mapping, Field and
  Data show each tool and your own projects in it, with a map of data from
  all tools.
- **Plans:** group projects across tools into an end-to-end workflow
  (see below).
- **Organisations and teams:** share plans with your team, or publicly.

## Plans: coordinating an end-to-end project

A plan links related projects so everyone can follow the data from imagery
to the final map. For example:

1. **Imagery:** the Drone TM project, and the resulting imagery in OAM.
2. **Digitisation:** the fAIr model and the Tasking Manager project.
3. **Field:** the Field-TM project or ChatMap map.
4. **Data and sharing:** Export Tool downloads and the uMap of the results.

Add projects by pasting their URL. Some third-party projects (MapSwipe,
Sketch Map Tool) can be added too. You can:

- Group projects into collections, reorder them and feature the important ones.
- Track the status of each project.
- Add placeholder tasks for future steps and link the projects later.
- Keep the plan private, share it with your organisation, or make it public.

## Disaster response dashboards (planned)

Portal's planned disaster response dashboard will use a plan per response
to link imagery, Tasking Manager projects, fAIr predictions, MapSwipe
validation and uMaps. Response teams can update these plans as needs change.

## Roadmap

As of October 2026 (see [roadmap.hotosm.org](https://roadmap.hotosm.org)
for current dates):

- **Done:** HOT Accounts login, tool menu, per-stage sections, plans,
  organisations, third-party projects in plans.
- **In progress:** an open mapping marketplace (find services or offer them
  as a vendor), public profiles, integration with the HOT Learning Center.
- **Planned:** disaster response dashboards, maps for plans,
  visualisation options, story maps and metrics.

## When not to use it

Portal links projects and data; it doesn't store or analyse data itself.
For a full geoportal or data catalogue, use GeoNode, or HDX for humanitarian
datasets.
